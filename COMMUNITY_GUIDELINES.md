# TYTO Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026

TYTO is a place to discover and organize stories, books, comics and manga. Help keep it useful, respectful and safe.

## Do

- Treat readers, creators and other users respectfully.
- Share accurate titles, credits, editions and source information.
- Submit only material you are authorized to share.
- Respect copyright, privacy and content warnings.
- Report broken, misleading, unsafe or rights-infringing material to the operator.

## Do not

- Harass, threaten, discriminate against or impersonate anyone.
- Publish private information without permission.
- Upload malware, scams, spam or deceptive files.
- Submit content that violates applicable law or Telegram rules.
- Claim ownership of another creator's work.
- Attempt to bypass moderation, administrator controls or technical restrictions.

## Moderation

The operator may review submissions, reject or remove content, limit features or restrict access when necessary to protect users, creators or the archive. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent restriction.

## Reporting

Send a concise report to [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot) with the relevant item or message reference and a description of the concern. For copyright complaints, follow [COPYRIGHT.md](COPYRIGHT.md).

---

Be curious. Give creators credit. Preserve stories responsibly.
