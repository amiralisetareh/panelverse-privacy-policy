# TYTO Copyright and Rights Policy

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026  
**Service:** [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot)

TYTO respects the rights of authors, artists, publishers, translators, studios and other rights holders. A work appearing in an archive or catalogue does not mean that TYTO owns it or that redistribution is authorized.

## 1. What may be submitted

Only submit or publish material that you:

- Own;
- Have a license or permission to use;
- Are otherwise legally authorized to archive or distribute; or
- May use because it is in the public domain or covered by a valid legal exception.

Do not assume that a work is public domain merely because it is old, available online, or shared by another account.

## 2. Report alleged infringement

A rights holder or authorized representative may submit a notice through [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot). Include:

1. Your name and a reliable contact method.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work.
3. The exact TYTO item, message or file at issue, including a link or identifying details.
4. A clear explanation of your ownership or authority to act.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate.

Do not send unnecessary identity documents or sensitive personal information.

## 3. Review and action

The operator may request clarification, temporarily restrict access to the identified material, remove material, or take other appropriate action after reviewing a sufficiently detailed report. A report does not guarantee a particular outcome or response time.

## 4. Counter-notice

If material was restricted because of a mistaken identification, the affected submitter may contact the operator with the item reference, an explanation and evidence of authorization. The operator may consider the response and restore material where appropriate and lawful.

## 5. Repeat or serious violations

The operator may restrict users or administrators who repeatedly submit infringing material or abuse the reporting process.

## 6. No legal determination

This policy is an operational reporting process, not a legal determination that any particular use is lawful or unlawful. Nothing here limits rights or remedies available under applicable law.

## 7. Contact

Send copyright reports to [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot), clearly marking the message **COPYRIGHT REPORT**.
