PanelVerse AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

PanelVerse AI is a Telegram bot for discovering, identifying, searching, and recommending manga and comics.

Information We Process

When you use PanelVerse AI, we may process information you voluntarily send to the bot, including Telegram messages, commands, images, and information required to provide the bot's features.

Images and AI Processing

Images submitted for comic or manga recognition may be processed by third-party artificial intelligence services to identify titles, characters, or related information.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate PanelVerse AI, identify comics and manga, provide recommendations, manage favorites, search the PanelVerse Archive, and provide requested bot features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the bot, including Telegram, AI providers, and hosting providers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate the service. You may request deletion of information associated with your use of PanelVerse AI by contacting the administrator.

Copyright and Files

PanelVerse AI is intended to provide access only to comics, manga, and other files that PanelVerse is authorized to distribute, including licensed, creator-authorized, or public-domain material.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as PanelVerse AI develops.
