# TYTO Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026  
**Service:** TYTO Telegram bot — [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot)  
**Operator:** The individual/project maintainer operating TYTO ("we", "us").

TYTO is a Telegram-based discovery and archive service for books, comics, manga and related media. This policy explains what information the bot may receive and how it is handled.

## 1. Information processed

When you interact with TYTO, Telegram makes certain information available to the bot. Depending on your interaction, this can include:

- Your Telegram numeric user ID.
- Your Telegram username, display name and language setting, if provided by Telegram.
- Messages, commands, callback selections and other content you send to the bot.
- Telegram file identifiers and metadata for files you submit or ask the bot to process.

The bot's current code also maintains an archive database containing catalogue metadata and uploaded archive records. The admin ingestion workflow records operational information such as filenames, MIME types, file sizes, processing status, timestamps and audit events. The admin workflow uses administrator identifiers such as `web-admin` in audit records.

The reviewed code does **not** establish that TYTO maintains a user profile database, stores a user's reading history, or sells user information. This policy does not claim those features exist.

## 2. Purposes

Information may be processed to:

- Receive and respond to Telegram commands and interactions.
- Search, organize and display catalogue or archive records.
- Process files submitted to the archive by authorized administrators.
- Prevent duplicate uploads, validate files and maintain archive quality.
- Secure and operate the service, investigate errors and maintain an audit trail.
- Respond to support, privacy or copyright requests.

## 3. Telegram and service providers

TYTO operates through Telegram. Your use of the bot is also subject to Telegram's own terms and privacy policy. Telegram processes information under its own policies and infrastructure.

The bot may rely on its hosting provider and storage environment to operate. The operator should keep access restricted and configure the host securely. No claim is made here that data is end-to-end encrypted between you and the bot operator.

## 4. Storage and retention

Archive catalogue records and uploaded files may be retained for as long as they are part of the archive or are needed for authorized operations. Operational logs and audit records may be retained for security, troubleshooting and accountability.

The exact retention period depends on the deployment and its storage configuration. Do not send passwords, payment details, government identifiers or other sensitive personal information to the bot.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to the operator and people authorized to administer the service, and may be processed by Telegram or infrastructure providers as necessary to run the bot.

Archive material may be published to a configured Telegram channel only through an administrator-controlled approval and publication workflow. Do not submit personal information in files intended for publication.

## 6. Your choices and requests

You may stop interacting with the bot at any time by blocking it. To ask what information may be associated with your interaction, or to request correction or deletion where technically and legally possible, contact the operator through [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot) and include your Telegram numeric ID if you know it.

A request may require reasonable verification. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution or integrity of the archive. Blocking the bot does not automatically erase records already stored.

## 7. Security

The operator uses access controls and an administrator authentication mechanism for the web archive interface. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep bot tokens, administrator tokens and hosting credentials private; never publish them in source control.

## 8. Children

TYTO is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the operator so the issue can be reviewed.

## 9. Changes

This policy may be updated as TYTO's features, hosting or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the TYTO operator via [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot).
